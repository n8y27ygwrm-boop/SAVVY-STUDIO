Eye

The people, the places, and what happens next — in one working view.

The Problem

The work happens in one place. The information lives in five others. Client details are scattered across CRMs, spreadsheets, and messaging threads. Territory addresses live in maps. Visit histories get buried in notes or forgotten.

The issue is the seam between them: a visit happens, conditions change, a follow-up is required — and by morning that context is lost. Eye holds that context between actions, so field operations proceed seamlessly.

The Product

Everything around the work, without leaving the work. Eye unifies client profiles, real-time territory maps, visit scheduling, and live follow-up queues into an integrated operational surface.

Operational Flow:

Client Account → Visit Log → Territory Map → Next Action

Core Capabilities & Value

  • Context Preservation: Client history, preferences, and visit notes stay attached to the account.
  • Territory Mapping: Plan routes and see the entire day's visits visually in real-time.
  • Definite Next Actions: Every interaction concludes with a concrete scheduled follow-up or task.
